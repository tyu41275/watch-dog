# Independent deliberation protocol and completion

Status: Completed for the approved synthesis
Completed: 2026-08-31

The product vision received an independent review against a frozen input packet. The traceable record, accepted findings, superseded recommendation, and unresolved delivery risks are preserved in [independent-deliberation.md](independent-deliberation.md).

The review ran read-only and independently from the vision author. The final product decision is authoritative where it conflicts with the review: both Paste Scan and a Watch Dog-owned same-origin Live Page Scan are protected initial-release modes, while arbitrary third-party active-tab scanning remains deferred.

No claim is made that multiple reviewers reached consensus. This is one independent critique plus an approved product synthesis.
